06 / QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

What does turnkey interior design include?

Turnkey interior design can bring the design direction, spatial planning, materials and finishes, furniture and styling, sourcing, procurement and installation together as one coordinated project. The exact scope is agreed around the requirements of each project.

Do you design residential and commercial spaces?

Yes. Armani Interiors is positioned around turnkey interior design for both residential and professional workspaces in Cape Town.

Where does Armani Interiors operate?

Armani Interiors is based around Cape Town, South Africa. Project availability and travel requirements can be discussed during an initial enquiry.

What types of properties do you work on?

The studio can discuss residential homes, apartments and professional/workspace environments. The appropriate scope is established during the project consultation.

How does the interior design process work?

The process is structured around consultation, briefing and concept development, design development, materials and finishes, procurement, installation and final styling and handover.

How do I start an interior design project?

Use the project enquiry form below or call 063 314 1801. Sharing the property type, intended scope, preferred timing and a short description of what you need will help begin the conversation.
